### Course Objectives

Upon completion of this course, participants will be able to:\
• Define how God’s love can transform your life.\
• Identify the characteristics of abusive relationships.\
• Recognize reoccurring coping patterns of behavior and develop alternative responses.\
• Reestablish new inner music and self-talk.\
• Describe medical impact surviving an abusive relationship.\
• Demonstrate the ability to rebuild self-esteem.\
• Build a personal relationship with God to gain knowledge, wisdom and understanding.\
• Create a new life-action plan using the seven measures of perfection.
