# Section 4: Build Your Self-Esteem

### Introduction

The next step to your recovery is to rebuild your self-esteem. Learning what self-esteem is and what causes low self-esteem will help you develop methods to enhance yours. Achieving a healthy level of self-esteem is very important. Understanding the pitfalls you may experience can guard you from setbacks. This is the beginning of a new journey for you and the first day of the rest of your life.
