# Section 3: Understanding Abusive Relationships

### Introduction

The next step to the recovery process is to identify how you got here. This Section helps you clearly understand what behavior is abusive and how to recognize the warning signs to protect yourself now, and in future relationships.

Upon completion of this Section, you will be able to:\
\
• Describe the status of current relationships.\
\

\
• Develop methods to overcome bitterness and forgiveness.\
\
• Identify types of abuse.\
\
• Define cycle of violence.
